What it is

Log a meal fast (photo optional, dishes seen before prefill) whether you ate out, cooked at home or ordered in. Search answers "have I eaten this?" with count, last time and place, average price and range, plate score and rating. Insights: variety score (Simpson diversity), plate score by day, spend split, taste radar, cuisines, top places. Share cards like Strava: Card / Receipt / Stats templates, Paper / Dark / Photo / Transparent backgrounds, Story 9:16 or Post 1:1. Taste buds: match % from taste profiles, tag buds in meals (+10), plan a hangout by WhatsApp, "We went" (+25), bud levels. Food-only photos (on-device cut-out), nutrition estimate + tips, Today bar vs goal, Lab recipes from typed / spoken / scanned ingredients, and a 5-step goal quiz (Mifflin-St Jeor).

Built to beat PlateDiary (private restaurant photo journal) and Mynu (home-cooking journal), in the same family as jeda (vs Sansa) and tally (share cards).

Honest limits: nutrition is an estimate from typical portions, not medical advice; no dish recognition from photos; food the exact tint of the plate can still be trimmed with it; buds are sample profiles on this device only (no server yet).
