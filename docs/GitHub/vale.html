From the README

You wake at the edge of an ancient fairy realm whose light is fading. The Fairy Elder asks you to recover five lost Lumenshards scattered across the Vale and bring them to the Sacred Glade at its heart. Each shard is guarded by a fairy keeper who will share a memory when spoken to. When all five shards are placed on the Glade's altar, the Vale's light returns.

VALE is a first-person fairy explorer built on Unity 6 LTS with HDRP 17.2. It is a small, polished prototype focused on atmosphere, collectible exploration, and character dialogue.
