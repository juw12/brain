# reda

> Reda - teman untuk biduran kronis (chronic hives companion, PWA)

- **Repo:** https://github.com/juw12/reda (public)
- **Live:** https://juw12.github.io/reda/ · demo: https://juw12.github.io/reda/?demo=1
- **Language:** JavaScript (vanilla ES modules, no build step)
- **Topics:** health, urticaria, pwa, offline-first, indonesia
- **Started:** 2026-11-10

## What it does

- **Today:** 30-second check-in (wheals 0-3, itch 0-3 = daily UAS), swelling and emergency toggles, dose ticks, next-step advice.
- **Patterns:** weekly UAS7 trend, 28-day calendar, trigger finder that compares bad and good days (same day and next day).
- **Self-tests:** scratch, ice cube, pressure, heat, exercise, sun - steps, timers and real reference photos (Wikimedia Commons, credited); "is this hives?" gallery incl. angioedema.
- **Care:** medicine plan, EAACI/GA²LEN treatment ladder with "you are here", what to avoid, comfort measures, doctor questions, printable report.
- Bahasa Indonesia first, English; data stays on the phone; works offline.

## Notes

- General information, not medical advice; the app never raises a dose on its own.
- UAS7 used instead of UCT (licensed). Jeda-family product shape with its own palette.
